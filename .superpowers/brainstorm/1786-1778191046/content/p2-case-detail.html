Access Control Policy

Version 2.1  ·  Last revised: 2026-03-10  ·  Owner: IT Security

1. Purpose

This policy establishes the requirements for granting and revoking access to company systems and data assets. It applies to all permanent and contract staff.

2. Scope

Applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors with system access. Temporary access is subject to a 90-day review cycle.

3. Responsibilities

The IT Security team is responsible for conducting quarterly access reviews of all privileged accounts.

Access rights must be revoked within 24 hours of employment termination. The HR department notifies IT Security via the offboarding checklist.

4. Evidence Retention

Records of access provisioning and deprovisioning must be retained for a period of 3 years in accordance with company record-keeping requirements.
