TRACE redesign rollout

Choose the visual direction for the app-wide rollout. Your current requirement is locked in: visual-only changes, same workflows and data, with crowded pages resized and re-spaced rather than rewritten.

Surface: enterprise app shell and operational dashboards
Audience: analysts reviewing regulations, controls, diagnostics, and reports
Single insight: TRACE should feel closer to the cleaner Vercel-style experience without losing KPMG structure
Primary action: make every page easier to scan and calmer to operate
Dominant brand device: dark blue TRACE shell with one focused summary band per page

Shell-first rollout

Shared nav, page rhythm, tighter spacing, larger data regions, calmer cards.

Recommended
  • Applies the Control Quality Analysis visual language across the app
  • Keeps operational pages white, dense, and readable instead of overly decorative
  • Best balance of “Vercel-like clean” and KPMG enterprise tone

Hero-forward rollout

Stronger gradient headers and more page drama throughout the product.

Higher brand impact
  • Looks closest to a glossy Vercel-style showcase
  • Feels more “launch page” than “working surface” on busy internal pages
  • Risks adding visual weight where the user mainly needs faster scanning

Minimal facelift

Sharper tokens, better spacing, lighter chrome, but the same page bones.

Lowest risk
  • Fastest to land across the app
  • Improves crowding, but may still feel too close to the current UI
  • Weakest match to the redesign intent from your Vercel reference
