Page treatment system

The same hero-forward language gets applied differently depending on the page type, so the app stays cohesive without making every page feel identical.

Dashboard and reporting pages

Use the strongest hero band. KPI cards get more height, charts get more room, and the page opens with one branded summary instead of many competing surfaces.

Insight-led

Library and diagnostic workbenches

Keep the hero shallower so the working surface starts sooner. Filters, lists, and detail panels stay familiar, but spacing, sizing, and typography reduce crowding.

Operational

Execution and upload workflows

Hero stays strong, but the CTA and status live inside it. Existing upload cards, action bars, and result modules keep their behavior and simply inherit the new shell and sizing rules.

Action-led
Dashboard family: `/`, `/reports`, and high-level summary pages get the most dramatic hero.
Library family: `/regulatory-library`, `/controls-library`, `/frameworks-library`, `/asset-registry` keep their current mechanics but move into a calmer, better-sized workbench layout.
Workflow family: `/control-testing`, `/regulatory-testing`, `/risk-assessment`, `/evidence-assessment`, `/issue-management`, and `/controls-diagnostics` keep their current steps while adopting the stronger hero and cleaner panel sizing.
